# Project guide for AI agents

CareShare is an iPhone-first iOS app for coordinating non-clinical support during the first 72 hours after hospital discharge. The iPad demo uses the same app build and iPhone layout.

## Where to start

- `README.md`: product purpose, users, and task-sharing concept.
- `HackathonNotes.md`: primary source for the challenge, speaker guidance, constraints, and judging rubric.
- `DESIGN.md`: proposed screens, task states, visual direction, mock payment flow, implementation outline, and acceptance criteria. Distinguishes confirmed requirements from proposals.
- `IdeationRecommendations.md`, when present locally: proposed directions, tradeoffs, MVP, and research links. Recommendations are not an approved product specification.

## Working guidance

- Optimize for the three equally weighted criteria: caregiver value, a working journey under ten minutes, and clear payment/business-model logic.
- Focus on non-clinical support; the hospital already supplies clinical discharge instructions.
- Mock payments and provider bookings only. Do not charge anyone, collect real payment details, or require a real payment account for the demo. Use fictional demo data.
- Distinguish facts, event notes, proposals, and simulations. A referral does not prove availability, funding, or completion.
- Keep scope small enough for a live demo. Consider affordability, language, accessibility, caregiver availability, and housing circumstances.
- Preserve the single-build iPhone/iPad demo decision unless the user changes it.
