# CareShare

**Your recovery, together.**

## Run the iOS demo

Open `CareShare.xcodeproj` in Xcode, select the **CareShare** scheme and an iPhone simulator, then Run. Tap **Explore the demo plan** to start. The same iPhone-targeted build runs on iPad in compatibility mode.

The first implementation includes Plan, Find Help, Circle, accepted task ownership, a $24 mock meal checkout, completion history, and local persistence. All people, services, bookings, and payments are fictional. See [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md) for the demo script, free Personal Team signing, architecture, tests, and limitations.

The product vision below includes capabilities, such as invitations and shared accounts, that are not connected in this local prototype.

CareShare is a task-sharing application designed to help patients manage everyday responsibilities after leaving the hospital. By connecting patients with family members, friends, and caregivers, CareShare makes it easier to coordinate support, track responsibilities, and ensure important recovery tasks don't get overlooked.

## The Problem

Leaving the hospital doesn't mean recovery is over.

Patients often return home with a long list of responsibilities, including picking up prescriptions, attending follow-up appointments, preparing meals, and completing household chores.

For patients with caregivers, coordinating these responsibilities can be confusing. Family members and friends may not know what needs to be done, who is responsible, or where help is still needed.

For patients without dedicated caregivers, finding and organizing support can be even more challenging.

**CareShare addresses this problem by making recovery responsibilities visible, organized, and easy to share.**

## Our Solution

CareShare provides a centralized platform where patients can create and manage recovery-related tasks while allowing trusted individuals to volunteer their assistance.

Rather than relying on scattered text messages, phone calls, or verbal agreements, patients and their support networks can see what needs to be done and who is helping.

### Key Features

**Task Management**
- Create tasks related to recovery and everyday needs.
- Add descriptions, due dates, and priorities.
- Mark tasks as completed.

**Shared Responsibilities**
- Invite family members, friends, and caregivers to participate.
- Allow supporters to claim available tasks.
- See who is responsible for each task.

**Unclaimed Task Tracking**
- Identify tasks that still need assistance.
- Highlight upcoming responsibilities without an assigned helper.
- Help patients recognize gaps in their support network.

**Patient Independence**
- Allow patients to manage their own recovery tasks.
- Support patients with or without dedicated caregivers.
- Give patients control over which responsibilities they share.

## How It Works

1. **Create a recovery plan:** The patient adds tasks they need to complete or receive assistance with.
2. **Invite supporters:** The patient shares access with trusted family members, friends, or caregivers.
3. **Claim responsibilities:** Supporters choose tasks they can help complete.
4. **Track progress:** Everyone can view task statuses and upcoming responsibilities.
5. **Identify unmet needs:** Unclaimed tasks remain visible so patients can arrange additional assistance.

## Example Use Case

A patient recovering from knee surgery returns home with several responsibilities:

| Task | Assigned To | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Pick up prescriptions | Daughter | Completed |
| Drive to physical therapy | Neighbor | Claimed |
| Grocery shopping | Unassigned | Needs Help |
| Prepare dinner | Friend | Claimed |
| Take out trash | Unassigned | Needs Help |

Through CareShare, the patient can quickly identify which needs are covered and which still require assistance.

## Who Is CareShare For?

**Patients:** Individuals recovering from hospitalization, surgery, illness, or injury who need help organizing daily responsibilities.

**Family and Friends:** People who want to provide support but may not know exactly what the patient needs.

**Caregivers:** Individuals coordinating recovery-related responsibilities with others.

CareShare is designed to work whether a patient has an established support network or is managing recovery more independently.

## Our Goal

Our goal is to reduce the organizational burden of post-hospital recovery by making it easier for patients to ask for help and for others to provide it.

We believe that recovering patients should be able to focus on getting better rather than figuring out who is handling their everyday responsibilities.

## Future Improvements

- Automated reminders for upcoming tasks.
- Notifications when tasks are claimed or completed.
- Suggested community resources for patients without available caregivers.
- Calendar integration for appointments and transportation.
- Recurring tasks for ongoing recovery needs.
- Accessibility improvements for older adults and patients with disabilities.

## Inspiration

CareShare was inspired by the challenges patients and caregivers experience when transitioning from hospital care to recovery at home.

The Massachusetts Health & Hospital Association's *After the Hospital: A Guide to Post-Acute Care* (October 2024) emphasizes the importance of care coordination, caregiver support, transportation, and assistance with daily activities during recovery.

CareShare aims to make coordinating these everyday responsibilities simpler and more accessible.

## Disclaimer

CareShare is intended to support non-clinical task coordination and is not a substitute for medical advice or clinical discharge instructions.
